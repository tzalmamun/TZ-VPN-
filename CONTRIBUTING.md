# Contributing to TZ VPN

Thanks for your interest in contributing.

- Open an issue before large changes.
- Keep pull requests focused and document user-visible changes.
- Test on a real Android device where possible.
- Never commit real WireGuard `.conf` files, private keys, QR codes, credentials, or personal data.
- Do not claim a feature works unless it is implemented and tested.
- Preserve the licenses and notices of third-party dependencies.
